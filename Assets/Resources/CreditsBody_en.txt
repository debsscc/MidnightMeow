<size=120%><b>SPECIAL THANKS</b></size>

To our family and friends, for putting up with yet another night of "just a little more."

To the professors who believed in the project from its very first draft, for their patience, guidance, and for pushing us to finish what we started.

To the playtesters, anonymous and not so anonymous — every death against the rat horde helped make the game better.
